Quick answer

PokéSurvivor is a free, fan-made Pokémon survival roguelite that runs in a modern web browser. You control a single partner, your active move fires automatically, and you survive waves of wild Pokémon while choosing upgrades. The roster covers 966 Pokémon across nine regions from Kanto to Paldea, with 64 moves, 59 move evolutions, 87 Legendaries, 18 types and online co-op for up to four players.

It is not an official Pokémon product. PokéSurvivor is an independent fan project, unaffiliated with Nintendo, GAME FREAK or The Pokémon Company.

What kind of game it is

One partner, an endless map, and a build you assemble as you go

PokéSurvivor is a survival roguelite, not a turn-based RPG. You take a single partner onto an endless map, survive escalating waves and shape a build through movement, automatic attacks and level-up choices. Each attempt gives you room to refine the approach.

⚡

Automatic attacks, deliberate movement

Your active move fires on its own — your job is positioning. Read enemy patterns, step out of marked attacks and keep an escape route open as the horde thickens.

◈

Types and weather shape your options

A move's type changes its damage against each enemy. Dual types combine matchup modifiers, and weather can strengthen or weaken particular types. Check both before committing to a build.

◉

Survive together in four-player co-op

Online rooms hold up to four players. Experience is shared across the team, and staying near a fallen teammate can pull them back into the fight.

How a run works

From choosing a partner to surviving the horde

The loop is short: pick a Pokémon, manage your active move, choose upgrades and always keep a way out.

Choose a partner and learn its attack

Start with a Pokémon you enjoy. Watch how its opening move reaches enemies, then give yourself room to learn the range and rhythm before approaching a crowd.

Practise switching your active move

Your selected move attacks automatically. Switch slots while the area is clear so you can answer a different enemy or matchup without hunting for the controls.

Collect experience and upgrade with a plan

Gather experience when the route is safe, then read your level-up choices. Strengthen a useful attack, check its evolution requirement, and add defensive options as the run demands.

Keep moving toward an open route

Watch the space ahead, avoid marked attacks and circle the edge of a horde. Turn wide when there is room, leave blocked paths early, and grab missed pickups only when it is safe.

Good questions

Things worth knowing before a first run

Short answers to the questions new players ask most often.

Is PokéSurvivor free to play?

Yes. It is a free fan game with no in-game purchases. Partners, tickets and upgrades are earned by playing.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in a modern browser with JavaScript enabled. Installing an app is optional.

Can I play on a phone or tablet?

Yes. Use landscape orientation, drag the movement stick to move and tap a skill slot to switch attacks. Desktop players can use keyboard and mouse.

Where should a new player start?

Learn movement and move switching first, then develop one reliable attack. Everything else — evolutions, rifts, currencies — builds on that foundation.

Will progress be saved?

Signing in to the game enables cloud saves and progress across devices. Guests can play immediately, but their progress is not saved.

How do attacks and move switching work?

The move in your active slot attacks automatically. Switching slots changes which attack fires. Bonds earned from legendary victories let you fuse moves so everything in the selected slot fires together.

How many Pokémon are there, and how are more unlocked?

966 across nine regions, including 29 Starters and 87 Legendaries. Additional partners come from regional gacha draws using tickets earned by playing.

What is a legendary rift?

A Rift leads to a legendary encounter in one of 18 type arenas. A dual-type legendary randomly uses one of its types for the arena. In co-op, the whole group enters together.

Does it use the usual Pokémon type multipliers?

No — the modifiers are softer: roughly ×1.6 for super-effective attacks, ×0.6 for resisted matchups and ×0.3 for immunity matchups. Dual-type factors multiply together up to about ×2.4, and weather can affect damage further.

Is this an official Pokémon project?

No. PokéSurvivor is an independent fan project, unaffiliated with Nintendo, GAME FREAK or The Pokémon Company. All Pokémon names and related marks belong to their respective owners.

The short version

PokéSurvivor takes the survival-roguelite loop — one character, automatic attacks, escalating waves — and rebuilds it around a single Pokémon partner. The appeal is the tension between a build you plan and a map you can never quite control: you choose the upgrades, but the horde decides how much room you get to use them. With 966 partners, 64 moves and 59 evolutions, there is enough variety to keep refining a run long after the first clear.
